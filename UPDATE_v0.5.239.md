# Solo Anime Studio v0.5.239 — Silk Wet now blends like watercolour

**Silk Blend → Wet mode** now blends the way our Soft Wet Watercolor brush looks:
- Colours **bleed softly** into each other over a wider wet area.
- Colours mix like **transparent pigment**: red into blue gives a deep violet, not a grey middle.
- **Darker wet edges**: pigment collects softly at the edge of the wet area, like a watercolour tide line.
- The wet edge is **soft and uneven**, so it blooms like wet paint instead of a round stamp.
- Gentle cloudy unevenness inside, like a watercolour wash. **Texture** adds watercolour granulation.
- **Wetness** controls how far the colour bleeds. **Blend Strength** controls how strong the edges are.

Silk is still a blender. It only uses colours already on the layer and never adds your brush colour. The darker edge is the layer's own colour made deeper, not a new colour.

Nothing else changed. Soft, Smudge and Dry are the same. Wet strokes saved with v0.5.238 still look exactly the same, and all other brushes and drawings are unchanged (byte-identical).
