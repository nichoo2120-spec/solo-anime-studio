# Solo Anime Studio 0.5.207

Brush Settings now uses a compact 420x540 floating panel (bounded by the window), a narrow section list, tighter controls and a small preview at the bottom. Long explanations move to section help: hover the ? button or click it to read. Important status messages remain visible.

Tip & texture includes compact preset thumbnails for Ink/Pencil/Eraser categories and existing paint thumbnails. A small visual dial adjusts the existing Ink tip angle and roundness, committing on release; numeric controls remain available. Brush rendering and saved preset semantics are unchanged.

Collapse the panel to a thin bar and restore it from its header. F4 toggles Brush Settings while its Brush/Eraser inspector is available; typing fields and modal dialogs keep their own keys. The selected-stroke width editor is also bounded to a smaller window with internal scrolling.

Validation checks panel bounds, unchanged canvas dimensions, tip preset availability, dial mouse/keyboard input, help, collapse/restore, F4, small-window overflow, and existing preset/import/width-editor workflows. Full existing drawing and desktop checks remain enabled.
