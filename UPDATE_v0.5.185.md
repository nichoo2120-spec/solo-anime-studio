# v0.5.185 Infinite drawing workspace

Choose **Show Outside Frame**. Zoom out until the shot is a thumbnail, pan in any direction and draw with the shot completely offscreen. **Fit canvas** returns to the shot. **Frame Only** shows the camera view.

Pencil, Solid Paint, Soft Paint and Hard Eraser work beyond the original page in Paint mode. Fill, Mix, vector and selection transforms use the original drawing surface. Outside strokes support undo and redo, save with the project and can appear in the output when the scene camera moves over them. The shot keeps its original resolution. Projects saved with this workspace open in v0.5.185 and later.
