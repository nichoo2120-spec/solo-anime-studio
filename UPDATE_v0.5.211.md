# Solo Anime Studio v0.5.211

Brush Settings now keeps its compact live sample updating during continuous slider movement. Preview frames use the latest settings without restarting the pending repaint.

The sample uses the drawing engine's brush raster and compositing. Mix and Eraser previews include local sample paint so strength, pickup, added paint, and erasing are visible. Pressure and texture controls explain when their related effect must be enabled. Dry bristle presets show their natural ending behavior rather than an unused ending-taper control.

Saved artwork, drawing behavior, and preset defaults are unchanged.

Validation: TypeScript; 143 rendered preview checks across 32 stock presets and five Mix brushes; continuous-drag checks for 11 settings, with multiple distinct rendered frames before pointer release; compact panel and graph checks at 1x–2x display scale; existing release verification workflow.
