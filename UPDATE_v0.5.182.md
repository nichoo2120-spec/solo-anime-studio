# v0.5.182 Draw outside the shot

Choose **Show Outside Frame** in the canvas toolbar or View menu to add white drawing space around the existing shot. **Frame Only** hides the surround. Outside drawing stays editable and is saved with the project.

PNG and PNG sequence exports keep the shot's original resolution by default. A camera move can bring outside artwork into the shot. Margins add 12.5% of each dimension per side, with a minimum of 24 pixels. Projects gain the expanded project format when margins are first added.
